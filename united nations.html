Key Experiences

What I Brought to the Table

Each committee, each debate, each co-authored resolution was a step toward building the world I believe in.

🌍

Youth Delegate, Tunisia

Officially represented the Republic of Tunisia in the United Nations Youth Delegate Program, carrying the voice of Tunisian youth to global humanitarian and diplomatic discussions. Engaged with policy experts, senior diplomats, and fellow delegates across three sessions.

Diplomacy
📜

Resolution Co-Author

Co-authored six formal UN resolutions addressing sustainable development goals, gender equality in STEM, and climate justice for developing nations. Each resolution went through committee debate, amendment, and formal vote — sharpening both my written and spoken advocacy.

Policy Writing
⚖️

Human Rights Advocate

Actively contributed to Human Rights Council discussions on access to education in conflict zones, digital rights for youth, and gender-based violence prevention frameworks. Presented position papers and engaged in cross-regional dialogue with delegates from over 40 nations.

Human Rights
🌱

Climate Action Committee

Served on the Climate Action and Sustainable Development committee, advocating for equitable climate financing for North African nations. Helped draft language recognizing the disproportionate climate burden on developing coastal communities — including Tunisia's Mediterranean coastline.

Climate Justice
🤝

Youth Network Builder

Built lasting relationships with UN officials, NGO representatives, and fellow youth delegates from Europe, the Gulf, Sub-Saharan Africa, and Asia. These networks continue to inform my approach to global challenges and have opened doors to collaborative humanitarian projects.

Networking
🎤

Public Speaker & Debater

Delivered formal speeches in committee sessions, participated in structured debates, and presented position papers in front of international audiences. Developed the ability to articulate complex policy positions clearly, confidently, and persuasively — skills I now apply in every professional context.

Public Speaking
